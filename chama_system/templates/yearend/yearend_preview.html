How Year-End Distribution & Retained Accumulation Works
1
Principal Back Guarantee

Each active member receives back 100% of their share contributions saved during the financial year.

2
Customizable Distributable Pool

Gross revenue from checked streams minus operational expenses equals the profit pool, shared proportionately to member savings.

3
Retained Accumulation

Unchecked revenue streams (like Registration Fees or Other Income) remain in the group treasury and continue accumulating for future financial years.
