Registration Fee Rate

This is a fixed amount that ALL members must pay when joining the group. Unlike welfare contributions or other fees, this ensures equal treatment across all members.

Example:
  • If you set 300 KES, all new members pay exactly 300 KES
  • Change the rate in the future to 400 KES, new members then pay 400 KES
  • Historical payments are tracked separately

The system will calculate total collected as: (Active members) × (Current rate)
