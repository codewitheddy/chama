Interest Type Guide
Flat Rate

Interest is charged once on the full principal, regardless of duration.

Example: KES 10,000 at 10% flat = KES 1,000 interest, total KES 11,000.

Reducing Balance – EMI

Equal monthly instalments. Interest recalculated each month on the outstanding balance.

Borrower pays same amount every month. Interest is higher early on.

Reducing Balance – Equal Principal

Same principal repaid every month. Interest charged on outstanding balance, so total instalment decreases each month.

First instalment is highest; last is lowest.

Emergency Loan

Set Interest Rate = 0% and tick Emergency Loan Product to configure an interest-free emergency product. The repayment period (duration) can be left at 1 month as a default, but the treasurer can adjust it freely when issuing the loan.
