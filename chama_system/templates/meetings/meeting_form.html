Chama Meeting Protocol
Auto-Attendance Pre-fill
When you schedule a meeting, all active Chama members are automatically recorded as present. The secretary only needs to record late or absent members.
Fines & Penalties
Meeting lateness and unexcused absenteeism can have automatic penalty rules applied and tracked directly in the financial records.
Export & Archival
Minutes saved with rich text formatting are fully exportable to professional PDF and DOCX documents with the Chama letterhead.
Quick Formatting Tips
  • Use Headings (H2 / H3) for key agenda discussions and resolutions.
  • Use Checklists for action items assigned to specific members.
  • Use Blockquotes to record official resolutions passed by member votes.
  • Click Expand at the bottom right of the editor for full-screen distraction-free minutes writing.
