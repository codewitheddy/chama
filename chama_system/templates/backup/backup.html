Backup Best Practices
  • Automatic vs Scheduled: Use Automatic for zero-configuration daily snapshots, or Scheduled if you need specific hours (e.g. every 6 hours) and a custom drive location (e.g. external USB/hard drive).
  • Secondary Storage: For disaster recovery, periodically copy backup archives to a secondary physical drive or cloud storage.
  • Admin Preservation: Restoring backups automatically preserves your current administrator login credentials across database transitions.
  • Safe Pruning: Retention limits keep your disk tidy by keeping only the most recent N copies.
