Version 1.1.0 (October 2026) — Dedicated Welfare Bank Accounts & Smart Upgrades
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[NEW FEATURES & ENHANCEMENTS]
• Dedicated Welfare Bank Account Segregation:
  - Bank accounts now feature a "Fund Purpose" classification:
    * General / Lending Fund (Core Chama operations)
    * Welfare Fund (Segregated Emergency & Benevolent reserves)
    * Registration Fee Fund
  - Financial safety invariant: Loans, restructuring, and general Chama expenses
    can strictly only be disbursed from General accounts, preventing accidental
    commingling of Welfare funds.
  - Live reconciliation engine: Tracks net Welfare collections vs disbursements
    and verifies that bank balances match the Welfare fund position exactly.

• Welfare Contributions Management & Direct Deposit:
  - Enhanced Welfare Contributions page (/welfare/contributions/) with a 5-card
    live financial KPI summary.
  - Added Dedicated Welfare Bank Account banner displaying live banked reserves,
    account details, and real-time reconciliation badges.
  - Added instant "Deposit Cash to Bank" modal right on the Welfare page,
    allowing treasurers to record bank deposits with quick amount increments
    (+500, +1,000, +2,000, +5,000, +10,000) and auto-reconciliation.

• Smart Update & Upgrade Data Preservation:
  - Complete data isolation: Installer updates strictly contain ZERO databases
    or user uploads, preventing accidental overwrites.
  - Interactive Installer: Setup now detects previous installations and asks
    whether you want to update (preserving all records) or perform a clean reinstall.
  - Automated migration engine: On update, existing member profiles, loan ledgers,
    welfare balances, and financial accounts are automatically picked up and updated.
